About the Player

Here's how the video player works — it's all by design for a clean viewing experience.

  • Controls auto-hide after 7 seconds while playing — move your mouse or tap to bring them back.
  • When you pause, a "Click to Resume" overlay appears — that's normal, just click to continue.
  • Double-tap left/right on mobile to skip 10 seconds.
  • Dark overlays protect the content and disappear as you interact.
