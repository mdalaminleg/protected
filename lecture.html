About the Player

Here's how the video player works — it's all by design for a clean viewing experience.

  • Controls fade after 5 seconds while playing — tap the screen or move your mouse to bring them back.
  • Tapping the video only shows the controls. To pause, use the pause button on the bar or the big one in the middle.
  • A dark "Connecting to server…" screen shows while the video loads or buffers — it disappears the moment the video plays.
  • Double-tap the left or right side of the video on mobile to skip 10 seconds.
