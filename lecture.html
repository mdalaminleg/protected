      About the Player

      Here's how the video player works — it's all by design for a clean viewing experience.

      • Controls hide while a lecture is playing — move your mouse or tap the video to bring them back.
      • Double-tap left/right to skip 10 seconds. Press and hold the video to play at 2× until you let go.
      • Use the speed and quality buttons in the control bar to adjust playback.
      • On a weak connection you'll see a "Poor network" message — switch to a better network or choose a lower quality.
